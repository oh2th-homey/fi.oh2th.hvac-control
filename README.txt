Stop maintaining heating schedules. Tell the app who is home, and optionally the energy prices and the outdoor temperature, and it sets the right temperature in every room: comfort when someone is home, eco when nobody is or when the price peaks. Doors and windows, boost, guests and long trips are handled without extra flows.

Group your thermostats, heaters on smart plugs, air conditioners and heat pumps into heating zones. The app keeps each device within its own limits and steps, and every zone shows a plain reason for what it does.
